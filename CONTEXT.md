# Character Unity

One character application with an Archive foundation and a Unity layer for
presentation, editing and sharing.

## Language

**Archive original**:
An accepted upstream card and its files, retained for historical use. User edits
never replace an Archive original.

**Archive version**:
A retained state of an upstream card's files. A later scrape may add a version
without erasing earlier versions.

**Library reference**:
A workspace's membership link to an Archive card. The original and its unchanged
files remain owned by Archive.
_Avoid_: Imported copy, editable original

**Favorite**:
A user's selection of a card for their active library. Favoriting an Archive card
creates library membership through a reference.

**Fork draft**:
An unsaved proposed edit derived from another card. Discarding it creates no
saved fork.

**Saved fork**:
A user's saved derivative, with its own changes and a link to its source version.

**Source identity**:
The upstream provider and record identity, including its known aliases.

**Content identity**:
The identity of a card's portable content and assets. Distinct source records or
saved forks may have equal content while retaining separate provenance.
