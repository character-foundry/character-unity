# Provenance

The normative target is Unity's internal read boundary in
`docs/archive-read-contract.md`, implemented 2026-10-11. Requirements derive from
the original unification plan's Archive ownership and immutable-original rules
and the two audits' read-side-effect and stable-selection findings.

All fixtures are generated synthetic data. No credentials, production metadata,
archived cards or live database files were copied. This suite makes no claim of
conformance to Foundry federation or an external character-format specification.
